## Why desktop context matters during interviews

A live interview is not just audio.
The interviewer may paste a coding prompt into a browser, ask you to read a system design requirement, show a product scenario, or refer to a shared document.
If your AI assistant only follows generic questions, it can miss the actual problem.

ExtraBrain is built around live desktop context.
That matters because many high-stakes interview moments happen on screen:

- A HackerRank-style coding prompt appears with constraints and examples.
- A CoderPad-style editor contains partial code and failing cases.
- A system design diagram evolves as the interviewer adds requirements.
- A product case includes metrics, screenshots, or user segments.
- A behavioral question refers back to your resume or earlier answer.

Screen-aware context helps the assistant respond to what is actually happening, not only to a rough transcript.
That does not remove your responsibility to solve the problem honestly.
It gives you a better way to organize what you see, ask clarifying questions, and explain your reasoning.

## Coding interview support: what a useful copilot should do

A useful AI coding interview assistant should not just dump a final answer.
A strong coding interview depends on communication, constraints, tests, and tradeoffs.
The candidate still needs to drive the conversation.

ExtraBrain can help with the parts that candidates often forget under pressure:

1. Restating the problem clearly.
2. Identifying inputs, outputs, and constraints.
3. Asking clarifying questions.
4. Proposing a brute-force approach first when useful.
5. Improving toward a better time or space complexity.
6. Naming edge cases.
7. Explaining why a data structure fits.
8. Preparing a walkthrough of the code.
9. Reviewing bugs or mismatched assumptions after the session.

For example, if the prompt is an LRU cache, the value is not only producing code.
The real value is remembering to explain why a hash map plus doubly linked list gives constant-time lookup and update.
ExtraBrain can help you keep that explanation organized while you speak.

That makes it a practical AI Apply alternative for people searching specifically for an AI coding copilot, not merely an application assistant.

### Choose ExtraBrain for coding interviews

Coding interviews are not only about producing final code.
They are also about explaining tradeoffs, asking clarifying questions, handling edge cases, and showing how you debug.

ExtraBrain can help you convert a visible prompt into an approach.
It can also help you explain that approach in a way that sounds like reasoning instead of reading.

A useful coding workflow looks like this:

1. Let the interviewer finish the prompt.
2. Use the transcript and screen context to summarize the problem.
3. Ask ExtraBrain for constraints, edge cases, and a simple initial approach.
4. Speak the approach in your own words.
5. Ask for complexity and potential failure cases before coding.
6. Use screenshots or screen context to review bugs when you get stuck.
7. After the interview, review the transcript and notes to improve for the next round.

That workflow keeps you engaged with the interviewer.
It also reduces the chance that the AI becomes a script instead of a coach.

### Choose ExtraBrain for system design interviews

System design interviews are hard because the answer is not a single answer.
You need to clarify requirements, define scale, make tradeoffs, draw components, explain bottlenecks, and respond to changing constraints.

ExtraBrain can help you structure the conversation while you remain responsible for the architecture.
It can suggest categories to cover, such as data model, API shape, storage, caching, queues, consistency, observability, and failure modes.
It can also help you generate follow-up questions when the prompt is ambiguous.

For example, if the interviewer asks you to design a notification system, ExtraBrain can help you remember to discuss:

- User preferences and unsubscribe rules.
- Delivery channels such as email, push, and in-app notifications.
- Queueing and retry behavior.
- Idempotency and duplicate prevention.
- Rate limits and abuse controls.
- Metrics, alerting, and failure recovery.

That kind of support is more valuable than a memorized answer.
It helps you keep the interview conversational.

### Choose ExtraBrain for behavioral interviews

Behavioral interviews can feel simple until you are under pressure.
You may know the story, but forget the point.
You may remember the conflict, but forget the result.
You may answer honestly, but without enough structure.

ExtraBrain can help you turn your own experience into concise answer outlines.
It can remind you to include the situation, task, action, result, and lesson.
It can also help you prepare follow-up details without inventing anything.

A strong behavioral interview assistant should make your real experience easier to express.
It should not fabricate stories or push you toward dishonest answers.

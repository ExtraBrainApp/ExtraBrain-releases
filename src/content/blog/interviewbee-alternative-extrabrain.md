## Discretion, screen sharing, and responsible use

Many people searching for an InterviewBee alternative are really asking whether the tool will interfere with screen sharing or meeting software.
ExtraBrain is designed to stay hidden from screen sharing and screen recording on major meeting tools.

But discretion is not permission.
You are responsible for following the rules of your interview, employer, school, meeting, assessment, or platform.

## Practical setup checklist

Before using any InterviewBee alternative in a serious interview, test your workflow in a realistic practice session.
A good tool should reduce stress, not create a new source of risk.

Use this checklist before the real session:

1. Confirm whether AI assistance, transcription, screenshots, or notes are allowed.
2. Test your microphone and transcription setup.
3. Decide whether you want local-first settings or an external AI provider.
4. Practice with the same meeting platform you expect to use.
5. Prepare a few personal stories for behavioral questions.
6. Practice explaining technical answers out loud instead of silently reading notes.
7. Review one practice transcript and identify what you need to improve.

This preparation matters more than any single feature.
The best AI interview assistant should help you sound more like yourself on a good day.
It should not turn you into someone who cannot explain their own work.

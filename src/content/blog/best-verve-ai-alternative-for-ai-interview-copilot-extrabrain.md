## A practical interview workflow with ExtraBrain

Here is a responsible workflow for using ExtraBrain as an AI interview copilot where assistance is allowed.

### Before the interview

Install and test ExtraBrain before the day of the interview.
Choose your transcription mode and AI provider.
If you want the most local posture, configure local Parakeet transcription and local Gemma 4 where installed and compatible.

Add the job description, your role context, and a few reminders about your strongest projects.

### During the interview

Use the live transcript to stay oriented.
Let the copilot help you identify the actual question, not just the last sentence spoken.
For technical rounds, use screen context to reason about the prompt, constraints, code, or diagram.

Review the transcript while the conversation is fresh.

Turn the session into preparation for the next round instead of letting it disappear into memory.

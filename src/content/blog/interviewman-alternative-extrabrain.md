## Main differences

### ExtraBrain is local-first instead of cloud-first by default

ExtraBrain gives users a local-first posture that is especially important for interview notes, transcripts, screenshots, and private career history.

## Real-time interview help

### Answer structure under pressure

The hardest part of many interviews is not knowing nothing.
It is knowing too much and saying it in the wrong order.

ExtraBrain can help turn live transcript and screen context into usable answer outlines.
For behavioral interviews, that might mean a STAR structure.
For system design rounds, that might mean requirements, constraints, API shape, storage, scaling, reliability, and tradeoffs.
For coding interviews, that might mean clarifying questions, edge cases, approach, complexity, and explanation.

This is where a good AI interview copilot should feel like a thinking aid rather than a script machine.
The goal is not to read a perfect answer word for word.
The goal is to stay calm enough to explain your real thinking.

### Coding and problem-solving support

For software engineering interviews, ExtraBrain is especially useful because it combines live context with technical support.
You can use it for coding interviews, debugging conversations, algorithm explanations, and system design rounds.

Useful coding support usually includes:

- Restating the problem in plain English.
- Identifying likely edge cases.
- Suggesting clarifying questions.
- Explaining time and space complexity.
- Breaking down a solution into steps.
- Helping you explain tradeoffs out loud.
- Reviewing what went wrong after the session.

That last point is underrated.

### Behavioral interview support

Behavioral interviews are often harder than they look.
You need to remember the right story, choose the right level of detail, sound natural, and connect the example to the role.

ExtraBrain can help generate STAR outlines from the live question and your own context.
For example, if the interviewer asks about conflict, leadership, ambiguity, or a failed project, the useful output is not a generic corporate answer.

- A short setup.
- A clear responsibility.

ExtraBrain can help you keep that structure in mind while still letting you speak in your own voice.

## A practical prompt setup for ExtraBrain

One of the best ways to get value from ExtraBrain is to create a role-specific profile before the interview.
Here is a practical example for a Salesforce interview.
Adapt it to your own role, resume, and job description.

```text
Role: You are a senior Salesforce solutions architect and interview coach.
Help me prepare for a high-stakes Salesforce interview using technical reasoning and STAR-method behavioral structure.

Context:
Use my resume, the job description, the live transcript, and visible screen context when available.
Identify gaps between my experience and the role requirements.
Prioritize answers that are honest, concise, and specific to my background.

For technical questions:
Explain architectural tradeoffs.
Call out governor-limit risks.
Break complex answers into small steps.
Help me explain why the approach is better than common alternatives.

For behavioral questions:
Use the STAR method.
Keep each answer outline short enough to speak naturally.
Suggest metrics only when they are supported by my real experience.
End with a reflection that shows judgment.

Output style:
Give me a short answer outline first.
Then give me optional follow-up details.
Mark anything I should verify before saying it out loud.
```

This kind of prompt works because it does not ask the AI to impersonate you.
It asks the AI to help organize your own context.

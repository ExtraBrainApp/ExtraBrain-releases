ExtraBrain can detect notable topics and questions in the transcript while a session is active. These appear as system cards in the transcript panel.

![Detected topic card during recording](/assets/screenshots/help/dark/main-recording-topic.png)

## What The Cards Mean

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| New Topic | ExtraBrain detected a topic worth analyzing or tracking. |
| New Question | ExtraBrain detected a question in the conversation. |
| Confidence | The estimated strength of the detection. |
| Analyze | Starts analysis using that detected topic or question as the focus. |

## Good Uses

- Capture a coding prompt after it is spoken.
- Track a system design requirement.
- Pull out a meeting question.
- Ask for a concise response to the latest topic.

Only the latest topic or question may show shortcut hints, depending on your shortcut settings.

## Detection Questions

### Why did ExtraBrain detect a topic instead of a question?

Topic and question cards depend on transcript context. A broad discussion may become a New Topic, while a direct spoken question may become a New Question.

### What should I do with a detected topic card?

Use **Analyze** when the topic is important enough to ask the selected AI provider for structure, tradeoffs, next steps, or a concise answer.

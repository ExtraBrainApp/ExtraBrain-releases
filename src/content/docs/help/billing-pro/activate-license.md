Open **Settings -> License** to activate or manage ExtraBrain Pro.

![ExtraBrain license settings tab](/assets/screenshots/help/dark/settings-license.png)

## Activate A License

1. Open **Settings -> License**.
2. Paste your license key.
3. Click **Activate**.
4. Wait for validation.
5. Confirm the Pro status appears.

Use the exact license key from your purchase or account email. Avoid adding spaces before or after the key, and keep the key private like any other account credential.

## After Activation

The License tab can show license status and a masked key. Pro can unlock advanced workflow controls such as custom profiles and premium model controls where enabled.

See [custom profiles](/help/billing-pro/custom-profiles/) if you want reusable prompt guidance for a specific workflow, interview style, or meeting format.

## Deactivate

Use deactivation when moving a license to another setup or removing Pro from the current Mac.

Provider usage costs remain separate from ExtraBrain Pro. OpenAI, Anthropic, Deepgram, Claude, Codex, or custom provider costs are governed by those providers.

## Activation Questions

### Why is my ExtraBrain Pro license not activating?

Check that the key is complete, pasted without extra whitespace, and intended for the current ExtraBrain account or device. Also confirm the Mac can reach the license validation service.

### What changes after ExtraBrain Pro activates?

The License tab should show active Pro status. Pro can unlock advanced workflow controls such as custom profiles and premium model controls where the current release exposes them.

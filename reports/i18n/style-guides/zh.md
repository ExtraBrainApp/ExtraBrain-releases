# Chinese Style Guide

- Locale: Simplified Chinese (`zh-CN`) for the `/zh/` route.
- Tone: polished, concise product Chinese. Prefer natural UX/help-center phrasing over direct English structure.
- Avoid word-for-word translations of idioms such as "local-first", "sticker price", "dry run", and "fallback".
- Keep technical claims precise, especially privacy, local storage, provider routing, and pricing.
- Preserve protected product/provider names, prices, URLs, placeholders, and Markdown/code spans exactly.

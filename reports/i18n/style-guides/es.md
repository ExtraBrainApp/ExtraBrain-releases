# Spanish Style Guide

- Locale: Spanish (`es`), neutral international Spanish.
- Tone: polished, direct, professional SaaS copy. Prefer clear product language over literal English structure.
- Use `tú`-neutral imperative sparingly; avoid region-specific slang.
- Translate concepts naturally: "copilot" can become an AI assistant/helper phrase when "copiloto" sounds too literal.
- Preserve protected product/provider names, prices, URLs, placeholders, and Markdown/code spans exactly.

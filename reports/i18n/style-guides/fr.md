# French Style Guide

- Locale: French (`fr`), neutral France/international product French.
- Tone: concise, polished, professional, and natural for SaaS marketing and help-center copy.
- Avoid literal English calques and heavy nominalizations when a simple French phrase is clearer.
- Translate concepts naturally: "local-first" should describe local/device-first behavior, not copy the compound structure.
- Preserve protected product/provider names, prices, URLs, placeholders, and Markdown/code spans exactly.

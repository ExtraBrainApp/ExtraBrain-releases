# Ukrainian Style Guide

- Locale: Ukrainian (`uk`).
- Tone: natural, polished Ukrainian product copy. Avoid Russian calques and literal English structure.
- Use established Ukrainian technology terms; do not overtranslate protected provider/product names.
- Translate concepts naturally: "posture", "sticker price", "workflow", "dry run", and "copilot" by meaning.
- Preserve protected product/provider names, prices, URLs, placeholders, and Markdown/code spans exactly.

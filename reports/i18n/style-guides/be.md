# Belarusian Style Guide

- Locale: Belarusian (`be`).
- Tone: natural, polished Belarusian product and help-center copy. Avoid Russian calques and machine-translation phrasing.
- Prefer clear Belarusian syntax over literal English structure.
- Translate concepts naturally: "copilot", "local-first", "sticker price", "posture", "workflow", and "dry run" must be adapted by meaning.
- Preserve protected product/provider names, prices, URLs, placeholders, and Markdown/code spans exactly.

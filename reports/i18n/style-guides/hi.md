# Hindi Style Guide

- Locale: Hindi (`hi`).
- Tone: clear, professional, and readable for product and help-center content.
- Use natural Hindi with accepted technical English terms where they are more understandable than forced translations.
- Avoid literal translations of idioms such as "sticker price", "dry run", "posture", and "workflow".
- Preserve protected product/provider names, prices, URLs, placeholders, and Markdown/code spans exactly.

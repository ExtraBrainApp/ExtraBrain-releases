# German Style Guide

- Locale: German (`de`), neutral product German.
- Tone: precise, professional, and readable. Prefer natural German sentence order over English calques.
- Use consistent formal product language; avoid overlong compounds when a phrase is clearer.
- Translate concepts naturally: "sticker price", "posture", "workflow", and "dry run" must be adapted by meaning.
- Preserve protected product/provider names, prices, URLs, placeholders, and Markdown/code spans exactly.

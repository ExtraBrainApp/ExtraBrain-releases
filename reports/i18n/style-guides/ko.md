# Korean Style Guide

- Locale: Korean (`ko`).
- Tone: polished Korean product copy, concise and respectful without sounding stiff.
- Avoid literal English constructions and awkward noun chains. Prefer natural Korean UX/help-center phrasing.
- Translate concepts naturally: "copilot", "local-first", "workflow", "fallback", and "posture" by meaning.
- Preserve protected product/provider names, prices, URLs, placeholders, and Markdown/code spans exactly.

# Portuguese Style Guide

- Locale: Portuguese (`pt`), neutral international Portuguese unless the English explicitly targets Brazil or Portugal.
- Tone: clear, polished, and professional for SaaS/product copy.
- Avoid literal English structure; prefer fluent Portuguese phrases that keep the product claim precise.
- Translate concepts naturally: "copilot" can be an AI assistant/helper phrase if the literal aviation metaphor sounds awkward.
- Preserve protected product/provider names, prices, URLs, placeholders, and Markdown/code spans exactly.

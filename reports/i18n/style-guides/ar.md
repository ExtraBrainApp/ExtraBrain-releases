# Arabic Style Guide

- Locale: Arabic (`ar`), Modern Standard Arabic.
- Tone: polished, professional, clear SaaS/help-center Arabic.
- Use natural RTL Arabic phrasing; avoid literal English word order and awkward calques.
- Translate concepts naturally: "copilot", "local-first", "workflow", "fallback", and "live support" by meaning and context.
- Preserve protected product/provider names, prices, URLs, placeholders, and Markdown/code spans exactly.

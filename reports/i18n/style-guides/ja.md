# Japanese Style Guide

- Locale: Japanese (`ja`).
- Tone: professional, concise, and natural for SaaS product and help-center content.
- Avoid literal English word order. Use natural Japanese phrasing and add implied subjects only when needed for clarity.
- Translate concepts naturally: "copilot", "workflow", "local-first", and "dry run" should read like product Japanese.
- Preserve protected product/provider names, prices, URLs, placeholders, and Markdown/code spans exactly.
